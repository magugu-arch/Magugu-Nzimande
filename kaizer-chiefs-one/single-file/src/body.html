Matchday · Sample

Your matchday, in one place

How fixtures, the Match Centre and your Ticket Wallet will work together on a matchday.

Demo Sample content · not club data

On a matchday the app is meant to do three things well: tell you what is happening, get you into the stadium, and let you share the moment.

Fixtures and the Match Centre will show verified information from the club’s licensed data feed, with a clear note whenever anything is out of date.

Your Ticket Wallet will open your tickets from the official ticketing provider. Entry codes are fetched at the gate and expire within minutes, so a screenshot cannot be reused.

This is sample copy written to review the layout. Approved club content replaces it before release.

Community rules

DraftVersion 2026-11-draft-1

Structure only. The club’s legal and privacy teams supply the final wording before release.

Share your own moments

Post only what you took yourself or have permission to share.

Respect people

Ask before you post anyone’s face. No hate, harassment, threats or private information.

Protect children

Never share a child’s image without a parent or guardian’s agreement. Moments that show children get an extra safeguarding review.

Help and contact

Where do fixtures and scores come from?

From the club’s licensed match data provider. If the feed is late or offline, the app says so rather than guessing.

Why can’t I screenshot my ticket?

Entry codes are issued at the moment you show them and expire within minutes, which protects you from ticket fraud.

Who sees my Fan Moments?

Only you, until a moderator approves them. You can remove a moment at any time unless it is held for a safeguarding review.

Support contacts pending

Ticketing, membership and app support contacts are added once the club confirms its support channels.
