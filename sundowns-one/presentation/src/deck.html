The strategy
04 / 28

The strategy on a page

Four levers of supporter revenue.
One app pulls all of them.

Supporter revenue is known supporters × how often they come × what each is worth, plus what partners pay to reach them. Each part of the app moves one of those four numbers, and each is measured.

More known
supporters

  • Join from a stadium QR code, a ticket or a friend’s invite
  • Guest mode first; sign up when it earns something
  • Every ticket and membership links to a profile

More matches
per supporter

  • A first-match guide and planner
  • Bring Your Crew and the Second Match Challenge
  • Passport stamps for every verified visit

More value
per supporter

  • Tickets, membership and the store in one place
  • Offers by segment, not by blanket discount
  • Renewal and win-back journeys

More value
for partners

  • Opted-in reach, counted rather than estimated
  • Offers and redemptions tracked to their source
  • Totals for partners; personal data stays with the club
North star

Repeat verified attendance: the supporter who comes back. Every feature, campaign and partner is judged by it.

Who it serves

Six supporters the app
is built to serve

Each supporter has a job they want done. The app does it for them, and the club gets a measurable result in return.

The matchday regular

Wants: a ticket and no queue.

App: the pass, gate and seat, Passport stamps.

Measure: matches attended

The first-timer

Wants: to find the gate, the seat and the food.

App: first-match guide, planner, stadium map.

Measure: a second verified visit

The family

Wants: a safe, easy day out.

App: family guide, accessible routes, meeting point.

Measure: family attendance

The member

Wants: to be recognised.

App: membership link, Passport, benefits.

Measure: renewals

The far-away Masandawana

Wants: to feel close from anywhere.

App: Match Centre, local kick-off times, watch parties.

Measure: active days a month

The lapsed supporter

Wants: a reason to come back.

App: a win-back note, the Second Match Challenge.

Measure: supporters reactivated

These segments are working hypotheses. Each is sized from the club’s ticketing, membership and app data in discovery, and campaigns are weighted to the segments that prove largest. Concept imagery, not club photography.

Lifecycle journeys

The right message at the right
moment, and never one too many

Service message: always sent Marketing message: only with consent

What supporters receive

Sample messages; the club sets final wording and timings. *WhatsApp switches on only once the club approves the provider, templates and consent wording.

Guardrails, built in

  • Consent recorded per channel and purpose, with wording and version
  • A global suppression list, checked again just before every send
  • Frequency caps and quiet hours
  • Every campaign approved by a second person
  • Service and safety messages never carry promotions
  • One-tap opt-out, honoured immediately

Partner value
19 / 28

Partner value

A known audience is worth more
to every partner

Sponsorship is sold on reach. The app lets the club sell results as well: how many opted-in supporters saw an experience, used it and came back.

Counted reach

  • Opted-in supporters by segment and region
  • Real numbers, not follower counts
  • Reported for every campaign

Proven response

  • Offers tracked from view to verified redemption
  • QR and link attribution, at Loftus and online
  • Holdout groups show what a campaign added

Useful inventory

  • Properties that make matchday easier
  • Every partner placement labelled as one
  • One partner moment on Today, never clutter

The club keeps
the data

  • Partners receive aggregated reports only
  • Personal data is never sold or handed over
  • Consent per purpose, under POPIA
  • Every partner and offer approved by the club
Principle

Partners buy access to an audience through the club, never the audience itself. That keeps supporters opted in.

Protection

How the investment is protected

RiskHow we manage it
Providers differ from our assumptionWeek-3 gate: providers, their APIs and test access are confirmed before build spend, and re-scoped with you if needed.
Partners sign slower than plannedWithout any partner, the base case still brings R2.81m a season, 97.5% of the first year’s cost. The rate card is tested with partners during discovery.
Low adoption after launchStadium QR codes, a gate briefing, a founding-supporter stamp and the eight-step calendar. Results are reviewed weekly.
Supporters feel over-messagedConsent per channel, frequency caps, quiet hours and one-tap opt-out. The opt-out rate is a reported measure.

Brand and image rightsOnly the club’s approved crest, marks and licensed photography ship. Concept imagery stays labelled until replaced.
