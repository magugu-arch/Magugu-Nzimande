About this concept

Sundowns One

The Yellow Nation. One home.

Concept prototype · built 14 November 2026

What this is

A concept supporter app for Mamelodi Sundowns, prepared by LegacyLeverage Creative Agency. It is not an official club product and has not been approved by the club.

It shows how one app could take a supporter from finding a match to coming back for the next one: tickets through the official seller, a matchday plan, the stadium, the Passport, the Fan Zone and the wider Yellow Nation.

What’s sample

  • Fixtures and resultsFictional opponents, dates set from today
  • TicketsNothing is sold. No ticket codes are made.
  • Passport points and rewardsNot redeemable
  • PartnersNone named or implied

What it promises

  • Your choices start offNo pre-ticked boxes. Partners never get your details.
  • A person for what mattersMoney, entry, safety, accessibility and your data
  • Sources on every answerOr an honest “I don’t know”
  • Works on any phoneLow-data mode, large text and offline use

Real names used: Mamelodi Sundowns, Loftus Versfeld Stadium and the club crest supplied for this pitch. All need club approval before any public use.
