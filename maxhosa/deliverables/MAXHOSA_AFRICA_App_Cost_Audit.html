What moves the number

DriverEffect
Commerce platform APIsReusable APIs save R1m – R3m. A platform without them adds about the same.
Events and ticketingAn existing system with an API saves most of the R0.40m – R0.75m events line.
CourierScan-based tracking through an aggregator is in this budget. A live map needs a same-day courier with a location feed, which is Phase 3 money.
Content readinessLate copy, photography or SKU mapping keeps a 5-person team waiting at about R0.9m a month.
ScopeDeferring the message centre and store locator saves R0.25m – R0.55m. Launching on iOS first saves little, because React Native shares most of the work.

Paying for it in steps

  1. Discovery at a fixed fee (R0.7m – R1.0m). It produces a costed backlog and a quote for Phase 1.
  2. Phase 1 against that quote, billed monthly, with a release-approval gate at the end.
  3. Phase 2 quoted after Phase 1's analytics review. Spend the 15% contingency only on approved changes.

Confidence: about ±30%. These figures become a quote only after discovery confirms the platform, payments, courier, booking and events systems. The full method and the completion audit (the app is about 30% complete) are in the project's docs: COST_AUDIT.md and COMPLETION_AUDIT.md.
