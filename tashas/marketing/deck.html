The opportunity

Why an app, and why it creates value beyond the booking

What asset does this money create, and how does it earn? The app is a booking system, a loyalty programme and a marketing channel in one. It compounds: every guest it gains can be reached again.

01

Own the relationship

Social reach is rented and rationed. A guest who opts in can be reached directly with a new menu, an event or a reward, at almost no cost per message.

02

Know who walked in

A walk-in guest can love their brunch and leave without Tashas ever knowing their name. Bookings, rewards and a table QR turn visits into known guests, with permission.

03

Fill the quieter hours

Café demand arrives in peaks. Targeted campaigns for weekday mornings and afternoons move guests into the gaps, without blanket discounts.

04

Protect the table

Deposits on larger or peak bookings, terms shown up front and a reminder before every visit protect the tables that matter most, café by café.

05

Evidence, not guesswork

Every link, QR code and campaign carries its source through to the booking, so Tashas sees which channel actually fills tables, and spends accordingly.

06

The brand in their pocket

Tashas’s photography, design and each café’s own “Inspired by” story, carried into an experience as considered as the rooms themselves.

The strategy on a page

Four levers of café growth. One app pulls all of them.

Café revenue is guests × visits × spend, less the cost of serving them. Each part of the app is there to move one of those four numbers, and each is measured.

+

More guests

  • Book any hour from Google, Instagram, WhatsApp or a QR code
  • Every booking invites the rest of the table
  • Vouchers bring in new guests, paid for by existing ones
×

More visits

  • Rewards that count every completed visit
  • Welcome, post-visit and win-back journeys
  • New menus and events announced to guests who said yes
↑

Better spend

  • Occasions noted at booking: birthdays, celebrations
  • Pre-paid event seats and gift vouchers
  • Campaigns that sell the right hour, not a discount
−

Lower cost to serve

  • Deposits and reminders protect against no-shows
  • Confirmations and changes without a phone call
  • Direct reach replaces repeat paid media
Positioning

For guests who treat a café visit as an occasion, Tashas is the café that remembers them. The app is how it remembers.

Tashas app · marketing strategyMarketing engine12 / 24

Marketing engine

Six ways the app markets Tashas, every day

An app is usually treated as a cost of service. Built well, it is the most efficient marketing channel a café group can own.

01

Every booking is an invitation

A table for six is one booker and five guests. “Invite your table” shares the café, the time and a calendar link by WhatsApp, so every booking introduces Tashas to new people.

02

Walk-ins become known guests

A QR code on the table and the receipt: “Join and earn points for today.” The visit is confirmed at the till, so points stay honest. The best moment to ask is when the guest is already happy.

03

Gifts that recruit

A “Gift a morning” voucher is a new guest, introduced and paid for by an existing one. Redeeming it brings them into the app.

04

One page, one action

Campaign pages for brunch, coffee, cake, gifts and events, each with one button and a tracked link for email, QR, social and WhatsApp.

05

Reach without rent

Push, in-app and email reach opted-in guests at almost no cost per message, with frequency caps and quiet hours so every message is welcome.

06

Found where guests look

Verified café pages with structured data for Google, linked to each Business Profile, and a well-made App Store listing. Rankings are earned and measured, never promised.

Tashas app · marketing strategyLaunch marketing16 / 24

Launch marketing

The first 90 days: seed, launch, compound

Launches fail quietly when the app is ready but the cafés are not. The plan starts with the team at the host stand, not with an advert.

Weeks −4 to 0

Seed

  • Team briefing and a host-stand script at every café
  • Table talkers and receipt QR codes, tracked per café
  • Booking links on each Google Business Profile, Instagram and the website
  • App Store and Google Play listings, screenshots and privacy labels
  • A first look for guests who already said yes to email
Days 1 to 30

Launch

  • The pilot café first, then every café
  • A founding-member welcome reward, value set by Tashas
  • “Scan to earn today” at every table
  • Launch content from the photography, with tracked links
  • A weekly review: fix whatever slows guests down
Days 31 to 90

Compound

  • Rotate the five campaigns by day, hour and season
  • Festive gifting with “Gift a morning” vouchers
  • Post-visit and win-back journeys on, after legal sign-off
  • A/B tests on landing copy, with holdout groups
  • A 90-day review: move spend to the channels that fill tables

Measurement

Measured monthly, from day one

Business outcomes, not vanity metrics. Baselines are measured in month one, targets are agreed with Tashas in discovery, and every estimate is labelled as one.

Booking
conversion

Confirmed bookings divided by qualified booking starts, by café, device and campaign.

No-show
rate

With and without a deposit, by café and time of day, so deposit rules are set on evidence.

Known-guest
share

The share of visits linked to a guest who said yes. The north-star measure of this strategy.

Repeat guest
rate

Opted-in guests with another completed visit within 90 days.

Opt-in and
opt-out

Consent gained and withdrawn, per channel. A rising opt-out rate means fewer, better messages.

revenue
