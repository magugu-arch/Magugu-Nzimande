# Quest4Best website — costing (South Africa, September 2026)

What this kind of work costs on the South African market today, based on published 2026 price
guides and freelance rate surveys (sources at the end). These are market estimates to help you
price or benchmark the work, not a quote. Amounts exclude VAT (add 15% if VAT-registered).

## 1. Where this website sits in the market

| Type of website (SA, 2026)                         | Typical once-off price |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Single landing page, template                      | R1,990 – R3,500        |
| Small-business site (5 pages, WordPress, template) | R5,590 – R16,900       |
| Corporate website                                  | R16,900 – R40,000      |
| Custom-built platform                              | R18,000 – R100,000+    |

This site is a **custom-coded, premium corporate one-pager**, not a template. It has its own
design system, pre-rendering, a validated enquiry form, full SEO setup, WCAG accessibility and a
security layer. That puts it in the upper corporate / lower custom band.

## 2. Effort and value of the work done

Professional equivalent effort, priced at South African freelance rates: general web developers
R350 – R900 per hour, senior React developers R700 – R1,200 per hour.

| Work package                                                                    | Hours       | At R700/h             | At R1,200/h            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | --------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Brief analysis, identity sheet applied, structure                               | 4 – 6       | R2,800 – R4,200       | R4,800 – R7,200        |
| Premium visual design, responsive (desktop, tablet, phone)                      | 14 – 20     | R9,800 – R14,000      | R16,800 – R24,000      |
| Front-end build: six sections, navigation, mobile menu                          | 16 – 22     | R11,200 – R15,400     | R19,200 – R26,400      |
| Photo preparation: WebP, responsive sizes, preview image, icons                 | 4 – 6       | R2,800 – R4,200       | R4,800 – R7,200        |
| Request-information form: validation, anti-spam, POPIA consent                  | 5 – 8       | R3,500 – R5,600       | R6,000 – R9,600        |
| Accessibility (WCAG AA) and speed (pre-rendering, Lighthouse 97–100)            | 6 – 10      | R4,200 – R7,000       | R7,200 – R12,000       |
| SEO: keyword plan, metadata, structured data, sitemap, Search Console readiness | 5 – 8       | R3,500 – R5,600       | R6,000 – R9,600        |
| Security layer: CSP, clickjacking guard, form hardening, headers                | 4 – 6       | R2,800 – R4,200       | R4,800 – R7,200        |
| Deployment pipeline and shareable single-file preview                           | 4 – 6       | R2,800 – R4,200       | R4,800 – R7,200        |
| Testing across devices, documentation (audit, SEO, security)                    | 4 – 6       | R2,800 – R4,200       | R4,800 – R7,200        |
| **Total**                                                                       | **66 – 98** | **R46,200 – R68,600** | **R79,200 – R117,600** |

**Fair market value for this build: roughly R55,000 – R95,000** (mid-senior freelancer). A
boutique agency would typically quote **R90,000 – R150,000+** for the same scope, because of
project management, account handling and overheads.

Not included above (separate line items if charged):

- **Logo redesign and corporate identity sheet:** commonly R5,000 – R25,000 in South Africa,
  depending on the designer and the number of concepts.
- **Copywriting:** the copy came with the brief. Professional copy is often R3,000 – R10,000 for
  a site this size.
- **Photography:** client-supplied.

## 3. Cost to finish (remaining ~18%)

About **3 – 5 hours** of work once the client supplies the outstanding items (email, domain,
vector logo, copy sign-off, LinkedIn links, company details): **R2,100 – R6,000**.

## 4. Running costs after launch

| Item                                          | Cost                                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `.co.za` domain                               | about R89 per year (from R49 at some registrars)                                 |
| Hosting (static site)                         | R0 on GitHub Pages or Netlify's free tier; R100 – R300/month for managed hosting |
| Email, Google Workspace Business Starter      | about R179 per user per month (USD-billed, moves with the rand)                  |
| Form service (optional)                       | free tier usually enough; paid plans roughly R150 – R400/month                   |
| Maintenance retainer (optional)               | typically R1,500 – R4,000/month (updates, checks, small changes)                 |
| SEO content, e.g. monthly articles (optional) | typically R3,000 – R10,000/month                                                 |

**Minimum to run it:** about R89 a year for the domain plus about R179 a month for one email
mailbox. Hosting can be free.
