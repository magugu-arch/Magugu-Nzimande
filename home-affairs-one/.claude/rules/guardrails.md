# Guardrails

The hard rules live in `/CLAUDE.md` at the root of `home-affairs-one/`. They come from the product
brief (§18 and Appendices A and F) and apply to every change, by people or by Claude Code:

- no invented DHA law, fees, requirements, statuses or timeframes — sample content, labelled
- synthetic people and tokens only; no real identity documents, biometrics or uploads
- no fake logos, seals, uniforms, certificates, ID cards, QR codes or barcodes
- mock adapters only; no secrets, production endpoints or live integrations
- AI explains approved guidance and never decides; nothing is submitted or attested automatically
- external channels carry a masked reference and a secure link, never case content
- wallet, biometrics, the service guide and partner integrations stay behind feature flags
- audit and logs carry opaque references, never personal information

Human approval is required before: schema migrations, destructive operations, deployment,
activating any external API, or changing security policy.
