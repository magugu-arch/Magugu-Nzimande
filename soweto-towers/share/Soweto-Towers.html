Safe hands

Scared? Good.

Being scared is part of it. Being unsure isn’t. Here is how the crew looks after you.

Weight limits
Bungee Jump and straightjacket 35–110 kg. SCAD Freefall 35–100 kg.
Age
Nobody under 12 goes up the tower. Under-18s need signed consent from a parent or guardian.
One at a time
No tandem jumps. It is always one jumper at a time.
Weather
Rain stops everything until the crew re-rigs, and lightning detectors bring everyone down straight away.
Who shouldn’t jump
  • Severe asthma
  • Epilepsy
  • Cardio or respiratory disorders
  • Hypertension
  • Skeletal weakness, joint or ligament problems
  • Pregnancy
  • Being under the influence of alcohol or other drugs
  • Any other medical condition that should stop you taking part

Questions

Asked and answered.

Are there age or weight limits?

Bungee and straightjacket jumps are for 35–110 kg, and SCAD freefall for 35–100 kg. Nobody under 12 goes up the tower, and under-18s need signed consent from a parent or guardian. Paintball players must be 12 or older.

When are you open?

Thursday 12:00 till sunset, and Friday to Sunday 10:00 till sunset. We are closed Monday to Wednesday.

Is there an entrance fee?

Yes, R20 per person at the gate. The site is cash-free for everyone’s safety, so please pay by card.

What should I wear and bring?

Comfortable clothes you can jump in (preferably not a skirt), closed shoes and sunscreen. Arrive about 20 minutes before your slot with your ticket on your phone.

What happens if it rains?

We stop all activities when rain starts. As soon as it stops, the crew re-rigs and we carry on. The towers have lightning detectors: if the alarm sounds, everyone at the top is brought down straight away.

Can I move or cancel my booking?

If rain or lightning stops jumps during your slot, you can move it to another day for free. For other changes, WhatsApp us with your booking reference at least 24 hours before your slot. Paintball bookings are for the date and time chosen: no-shows forfeit payment.

Can we jump together (tandem)?

No. Tandem jumps cause injuries, so it is always one jumper at a time.

How much are videos and photos?

The full HD video is R170 and the photo pack R100, per person per activity. Video is already included with SCAD freefall and the straightjacket bungee. Jump + Film bundles the bungee and the video for R760.

Can I film with my own GoPro or phone?

Not on the bridge or in the tower: we can’t take responsibility for loose equipment at height. Instead, every jumper gets a free 10-second Scream Clip, and our multimedia crew can film the whole jump in HD.

Can we bring food, drinks or a braai?

We don’t allow outside catering or braais, but Chaf-Pozi restaurant is on site for drinks, lunch and dinner. Birthday cake is welcome: just leave the site as clean as you found it.

Do you offer group discounts?

Groups of 6 or more pay R580 each for the bungee (R50 off) and R360 each for SCAD (R40 off), applied automatically when you book. Lift rides are R70 each for 10 or more.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes. Gift a Jump vouchers are valid for 365 days and arrive by WhatsApp or email, or you can print them. The recipient enters the code when they book a timed slot.
