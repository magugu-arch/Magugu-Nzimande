01The idea

Two towers.
One bridge.
One line.

Soweto Towers is two cooling towers joined by a suspension bridge, with a 100 m jump between them. The identity is built the same way: three parts, drawn with one line, and a single red kept for the drop.

The name

Both T’s in Soweto Towers are drawn as cooling towers: a waist, a flared base, and a crossbar resting on the rim.

The monogram

An S whose top stroke runs on as the T’s crossbar: the bridge. The red stem is the drop, stopping short of the ground.

The Loop

The two rims seen from above, joined by the bridge. One line. Turn it upright and it becomes an S.

02The name

Two T’s, two towers.

There are exactly two T’s in Soweto Towers, and the towers stand in them. Each stem curves in to a waist and flares to its base like a cooling tower, and the crossbar rests on it with one fine cut, like the bridge on a rim.

Set in Archivo Medium, extended to 118%, with +16% tracking. The name is supplied as artwork. Never retype it.

03The monogram

Soweto, bridged to Towers.

  1. Two circles

    The S is drawn from two circles, the upper slightly smaller (0.89), so it sits upright and doesn’t look top-heavy.

  2. One line

    The S and the crossbar are one continuous stroke of one weight, with round ends.

  3. One cut

    The stem hangs half a line width below the bridge. The same cut appears in the name and the Loop.

  4. The drop

    The stem is the only red. It stops short of the baseline, like the cord at full stretch.

04The Loop

Seen from above.

From the air, the towers are two rims and the bridge runs between them. The Loop is that view, drawn in the same line as the monogram: two rims, one bridge, one cut where the line meets itself.

Upright, it is an S. The bridge climbs left to right, so the Loop turned on end reads as the S of Soweto.

The Loop is a graphic device, not a logo. It never replaces the name or the monogram. Use it as a pattern, a stamp, a frame, or in motion.

06Clear space and size

Give it room.

Keep a clear space of x on every side of any lockup, where x is twice the line weight of the monogram. Nothing enters it.

Horizontal30 mm · 120 px wide
Stacked22 mm · 88 px wide
Name35 mm · 140 px wide
Monogram5 mm · 16 px tall

The monogram is a single heavy line, so it holds down to a browser tab without a separate small-size version.

10The Loop as pattern

Tower Black · for night, packaging, the back of a card
Drop Red · wristbands, tickets, event moments
Bone · backgrounds, tissue paper, stationery

A pattern of S’s and views from above.

The Loop and its upright S alternate on a square grid. Keep the pattern tone-on-tone, within about 5% of the background, so type and logos stay clear on top of it.

Supplied as a seamless tile: pattern/loop-pattern-tile-*.svg

11Pictograms

Drawn with the same line.

Six activity pictograms on a 48-unit grid, with one line weight (3.6) and round ends, like the monogram. Red always marks the person or the moment.
