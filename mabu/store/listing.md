## Description (both stores)

African Luxury. Refined by Fire.

Mábu is a contemporary African luxury restaurant at Waterfall Wilds, Midrand. The app is the easiest way to
join us.

BOOK YOUR TABLE
Choose a date, a time and your party size, and your table is confirmed in moments. Tell us about an occasion,
dietary needs or seating, and we will be ready for you. Change or cancel in the app, and join the waitlist
when a night is full.

THE MENU AND WINE LIST
Browse signature cuts, seafood, vegetarian dishes and desserts, with photographs, ingredients and verified
dietary information, and the wine list by style.

EVENTS AND EXPERIENCES
Wine pairings, chef's evenings and tasting menus. Reserve your place and keep your ticket in the app.

MÁBU REWARDS
Earn with every visit and experience. Privileges, not discounts.

GIFT VOUCHERS
Give an evening at Mábu, delivered by email in moments.

PRIVATE FUNCTIONS
From private dining to exclusive venue hire, send an enquiry to our events team.

## Privacy answers (App Store privacy label / Play data safety)

Collected and linked to the guest, used for app functionality only, never sold or used for tracking:

- Contact info: name, email address, phone number
- User content: booking notes, dietary and accessibility notes, occasions
- Purchases: voucher and event purchase history (card details are handled by the payment provider)
- Identifiers: push notification token

Not collected: location, contacts, photos, browsing history, advertising identifiers. Guests can delete their
account in the app (Profile → Your details). Privacy notice: in the app under Profile → Privacy notice, and to
be published at maburestaurant.com/privacy.
