It is not an official Bulls product and is not connected to live club, ticketing, payment or stadium systems.           Golden Ticket Drop

          Open

          Win a ticket to a future home match. For supporters checked in at today’s match.

          Rules, eligibility and draw method
          • Eligibility: verified ticket and successful gate check-in at today’s match. Under-18s need guardian consent.
          • One entry per verified ticket. No purchase necessary.
          • Window: open now until the 70th minute (demo).
          • Prize: one ticket to a future Bulls home match (demo prize, subject to availability).
          • Draw: an authorised operator runs a random draw. The seed and result are recorded in the audit log.
          • The winner is validated against ticket and attendance before any announcement. Seats are never revealed before validation.
          • Winning is not guaranteed.

                Concept notice · not legal advice

                Privacy, in plain language.

                A plain-language draft of how Bulls One would handle personal information under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). The club’s Information Officer and legal team must review it before launch.

                What we collect and why

                InformationWhy
                Name, email or phone (account)To run your account and send essential matchday messages.
                Ticket reference, block, row and seatTo show your pass, guide you to your seat and confirm Fan Cam eligibility. We never store the raw ticket barcode.
                Gate check-in (from the stadium system)To confirm attendance for Fan Cam, rewards and prize draws. We don’t use GPS or Wi-Fi to decide you’re at your seat.
                Fan Cam photo and display nameOnly for the uses you switch on: stadium screen, app gallery, club marketing. Each is a separate choice.
                Points, badges and redemptionsTo run Bulls Rewards. Kept as a ledger so every change can be checked.

                What we never do

                • No facial recognition, face matching, emotion detection or biometric templates.
                • No publishing your phone, email, full name, ticket barcode or exact seat on a public screen.
                • No selling or sharing your personal data with sponsors. They get aggregated reports only.
                • Nothing goes to a stadium screen without a human operator’s approval.

                How long we keep it (proposed defaults)

                ItemRetention
                Raw Fan Cam uploadsDeleted 72 hours after the match
                Rejected submissionsDeleted within 7 days
                Approved screen creative30 days, or longer only with marketing consent
                Consent records and audit logsAs long as legally required to show what was agreed

                Your rights

                You can ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct it, delete it or stop using it. You can withdraw consent at any time in the consent centre. A moment already shown live in the stadium can’t be un-shown, but we will stop any future use.

                Children

                Photos that include anyone under 18 need a parent’s or guardian’s permission. Without valid consent, they aren’t shown publicly.

                Reference: Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013. The concept wording is v1.2 (demo). It is not a certified legal document.
