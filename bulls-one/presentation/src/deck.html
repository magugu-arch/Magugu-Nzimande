Executive summary

One app. An owned channel that
fills Loftus and brings fans back.

The Bulls have a famous stadium, a loyal following and a matchday like no other. Bulls One turns that into an owned, measurable relationship with every supporter who says yes, so each ticket, match and moment becomes the start of the next.

R3 450 000
to prove it

Fan Cam pilot, likely case excluding VAT. A web app, QR check-in and one screen at one home match, in 4–5 months.

R8 395 000 for the
full matchday app

Recommended scope: iOS, Android and web, ticket-linked check-in, live screen playout and rewards, in 8–10 months.

About R60 per supporter
per match to pay back

At 10 000 active supporters per home match (illustrative), that covers the low running cost and recovers the build in three seasons.

App, code
and data: the club’s

Transferred to the Bulls on final payment. Supporter data is collected under POPIA, with a clear yes for marketing.

R2.2m–R4.7m
a year to run

Support, hosting, messaging and match data. Lower if live data is covered by the club’s competition rights.

A pilot match
in March 2027

Dragons (20 March) or Edinburgh (27 March) at Loftus. Nothing beyond the pilot is spent until it proves the idea.

The opportunity

Why an app, and why it creates
value beyond the ticket

What asset does this money create, and how does it earn? Bulls One is a matchday companion, a loyalty programme and a marketing channel in one. It compounds: every supporter it gains can be reached again.

01

Own the relationship

Social reach is rented and rationed. A supporter who opts in can be reached directly with a fixture, an offer or a reward, at almost no cost per message.

02

Know who is in the stadium

A ticket bought for a friend leaves the club without a name. Ticket linking and gate check-in turn attendance into known supporters, with permission.

03

Fill Loftus beyond
the big matches

Demand peaks for the marquee fixtures. Targeted campaigns for families, students and lapsed supporters fill the quieter ones, without blanket discounts.

04

Put supporters on
the big screen

Fan Cam turns a photo into a moment the whole stadium sees. Moderated and consented, it is the most shareable thing a matchday can offer.

05

Evidence for partners

Check-ins, redemptions and screen moments are counted, not estimated, so sponsor inventory is sold and renewed on proof.

06

The club in their pocket

Fixtures, live scores, the pass and rewards in one place: the Bulls brand every day of the week, not only on matchday.

The strategy on a page

Four levers of matchday growth.
One app pulls all of them.

Matchday revenue is supporters × matches attended × spend per match, less the cost of serving them. Each part of the app is there to move one of those four numbers, and each is measured.

More supporters

  • “Invite your crew” shares every ticket by WhatsApp
  • Gift-a-matchday vouchers bring in new fans
  • Campaigns for families, students and first-timers

More matchdays

  • Points for every verified attendance
  • Kick-off reminders and fixture alerts
  • Win-back for supporters who go quiet

Better spend

  • Food and drink combos and pre-order
  • Merchandise drops and member prices
  • Hospitality and seat upgrades

Lower cost
to serve

  • A digital pass and smoother gates
  • Wayfinding and answers without a helpdesk
  • Direct reach replaces repeat paid media
Positioning

For supporters who treat a match as an occasion, the Bulls are the club that knows them by name. Bulls One is how it remembers.

Marketing engine
13 / 26

Marketing engine

Six ways the app markets
the Bulls, every day

An app is usually treated as a cost of service. Built well, it is the most efficient marketing channel a club can own.

01

Every ticket is an invitation

A ticket is rarely for one. “Invite your crew” shares the match, the kick-off and a calendar link by WhatsApp, so every purchase introduces the app to new people.

02

The big screen goes home

An approved Fan Cam moment arrives as a shareable card. Every share carries the Bulls One link to people who were not at Loftus.

03

Gifts that recruit

A “gift a matchday” voucher is a new supporter, introduced and paid for by an existing one. Redeeming it brings them into the app.

04

One page, one action

Campaign pages for the big match, family day and student nights, each with one button and a tracked link for email, QR, social and WhatsApp.

05

Reach without rent

Push, in-app and email reach opted-in supporters at almost no cost per message, with frequency caps and quiet hours so every message is welcome.

06

Found where fans look

Rich link previews on WhatsApp and social, fixture data structured for Google and a well-made store listing. Rankings are earned and measured, never promised.

Lifecycle journeys

The right message at the right
moment, and never one too many

Service message: always sent Marketing message: only with consent

What supporters receive

Sample messages; final wording, reward values and timings are set by the club. *WhatsApp and SMS switch on only once the club approves the provider, the sender, the templates and the consent wording.

Guardrails, built in

  • Consent recorded per channel and purpose, with its wording and version
  • A suppression list, checked again just before every send
  • Frequency caps and quiet hours
  • Every campaign approved by a second person
  • Service messages never carry promotions
  • Under-18s: no marketing, and a guardian’s consent for Fan Cam
  • One-tap opt-out, honoured immediately

Launch marketing
18 / 26

Launch marketing

The first 90 days: seed,
launch, compound

Launches fail quietly when the app is ready but the stadium is not. The plan starts with the stewards at the gates and the screen team, not with an advert.

Weeks −4 to 0

Seed

  • Steward and screen-team briefing, and a gate script
  • QR codes at gates, seats and concessions, tracked per spot
  • Links from the club website, socials and WhatsApp channel
  • A short link to install the web app; store listings follow the native apps

Pilot match · days 1 to 30

Launch

  • Fan Cam on one screen at one home match
  • A founding-member reward, value set by the club
  • “Scan at the gate to earn” at every entrance
  • Launch content from the night’s approved moments

Days 31 to 90

Compound

  • Rotate the five campaigns by fixture
  • Post-match and win-back journeys, after legal sign-off
  • A/B tests on campaign pages, with holdout groups
  • A 90-day review: move spend to what sells tickets

Return on investment

The app pays its way at about
R60 per supporter per match

Break-even is simple arithmetic: the running cost plus the build spread over three seasons, set against the extra contribution app supporters bring. The table shows a season’s contribution across a range.

Season contribution = active supporters × 9 home matches × extra contribution each. Contribution is the margin on tickets, food, drink and merchandise that would not have happened without the app, measured with holdout groups. Shaded cells clear the R5.0m target. The values are illustrative, not the club’s figures, and are replaced with real numbers in discovery. Sponsorship is upside and not counted.

In the middle case:
R5.4m a season

The target is about R5.0m a season: R2.2m to run the app (low case) plus R2.8m to recover the R8.4m build over three seasons. 10 000 active supporters at R60 each per home match clears it.

Measurement

Measured every match,
from the pilot on

Club outcomes, not vanity metrics. Baselines come from the pilot match, targets are agreed with the club in discovery, and every estimate is labelled as one.

Gate check-in
rate

Checked-in tickets divided by tickets linked in the app, by gate and by kick-off time.

Fan Cam
funnel

Submissions, approval rate and time to screen, so moderation is staffed on evidence.

Known-supporter
share

The share of attendance linked to a supporter who said yes. The north-star measure of this strategy.

Repeat
attendance

Opted-in supporters who return for another home match in the same season.

Opt-in and
opt-out

Consent gained and withdrawn, per channel. A rising opt-out rate means fewer, better messages.

Incremental
revenue

Proven with holdout groups and agreed attribution windows, never claimed from last click alone.
