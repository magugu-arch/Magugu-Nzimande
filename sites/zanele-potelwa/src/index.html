01 / About

Trust earned on air, on screen and on stage.

Zanele Potelwa is a South African radio and television presenter, live host and media personality. Weekday afternoons she co-hosts 5 Drive on 5FM. On television she hosts Tropika Island of Treasure, with presenting credits on Expresso Morning Show and Selimathunzi.

In 2026 she co-hosted the 32nd South African Music Awards at Sun City and was nominated for Best TV Presenter at the SAFTAs. The SA Style Awards named her The Next Big Thing.

Bringing people closer to the stories, people, places, style and experiences shaping contemporary African life, with warmth, curiosity, energy and purpose.
