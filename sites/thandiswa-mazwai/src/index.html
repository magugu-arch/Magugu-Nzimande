01 / The story

The past is not behind us. It is part of the song.

Thandiswa Mazwai is a South African singer-songwriter whose music brings traditional Xhosa rhythms into conversation with kwaito, jazz, reggae, funk and contemporary African sounds. From the pioneering kwaito group Bongo Maffin to her solo career, her work has made room for both movement and meaning.

Her sound is an ongoing conversation with history — a space for Blackness, Africanness, freedom, social justice and joy. It does not keep heritage behind glass. It lets heritage live, change and speak to the present.

“Music gave me an opportunity to feed my revolutionary self.”

— Thandiswa Mazwai · official artist biography

Featured release / 2024

Sankofa

A word from the Twi language of Ghana meaning “to go back and fetch what has been left behind.” The album connects archival Xhosa field recordings with jazz and West African music — placing memory at the centre of a living, forward-looking sound.
