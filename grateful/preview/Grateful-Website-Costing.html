This is what it would cost to commission the Grateful website from scratch in Johannesburg in 2026: the same pages, booking engine, PayFast payments, email, studio dashboard and search optimisation, built and tested to the same standard. Monthly running costs are listed separately.

To finish this site from where it stands

This build is 80% complete. Almost all of the remaining 20% is content from the studio and opening accounts, which are not developer costs.

Developer time still needed: about 11–18 hours, R4,950 – R17,100. Setting up Supabase, PayFast, Resend and Vercel; connecting the domain; verifying Google Search Console and submitting the sitemap; a PayFast sandbox test; testing on real phones; and the changes from the studio’s review. After that, only the monthly running costs of about R744 remain, plus PayFast’s fee on each payment.

Not included in these figures

  • PhotographyAn editorial fashion shoot. Get quotes from photographers, and confirm image rights in writing
  • Logo and brand identityAlready exists in the CI manual
  • Advertising, ongoing SEO campaigns and social media managementThe on-site search optimisation is included; paid ads and monthly SEO work are not
  • VAT at 15%Added if the supplier is VAT-registered. The R312k budget becomes R359k

How this was worked out

Hours are an estimate for a competent South African team building this exact scope from scratch, measured against the finished code. Hourly rates are placed within published 2026 South African ranges: freelancers R350–R900 (senior React and Node developers R700–R1,200) and agencies R450–R1,200. Actual quotes vary by supplier and by how much scope is trimmed. Service prices and the PayFast fee are the providers’ published 2026 rates. The rand amounts for dollar-billed services use R16.30 to the dollar.
