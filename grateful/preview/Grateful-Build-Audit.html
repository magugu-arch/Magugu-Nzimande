How the percentage was worked out

Each workstream's weight reflects its share of a launched, usable website. Its progress is the share of its checklist that is done and verified. Code counts only once tested, and anything that needs an outside account counts as not done. The total is the weighted sum: 22×0.95 + 14×1.00 + 12×0.75 + 15×0.35 + 10×0.65 + 10×0.96 + 10×0.97 + 7×0.78 = 80.4, shown as 80%.
