Chapter 03 — The Flagship

The Flagship

The House That Andile Built

The build series, professionalised into appointment viewing. A fixed weekly cadence, a format the audience learns, and an edit that treats the house like the main character.

Watch the Flagship {{ICON_ARROW}}
{{img IMG_6905 focus="35% 30%"}}
{{img IMG_6907 focus="55% 35%"}}
{{img IMG_6906 focus="50% 50%"}}

  • 01

    Weekly production cadence

    A fixed weekly release rhythm so the build becomes appointment viewing rather than occasional posting.

  • 02

    Format bible

    One document that fixes the structure, tone, segments and rules of the show, so every episode is recognisably the same series.

  • 03

    Recurring segments

    Repeatable segments the audience learns to expect — and that partners can attach to without interrupting the story.

  • 04

    Milestones

    Build milestones mark the season's shape and give the series natural moments to promote, sponsor and celebrate.

  • 05

    Human subplots

    The people around the build — trades, advisers, family where approved — carry the story between milestones.

  • 06

    Cinematic edit

    Long-form episodes cut with the care of broadcast television: considered pacing, grade and sound.

  • 07

    Short-form multiplier

    Each shoot yields clips, vertical edits and milestone moments for every platform, multiplying reach from one production day.
